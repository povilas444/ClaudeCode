Impact

More movement, fewer barriers

What usually holds people back is not motivation but uncertainty: where to look, who to trust and what the price includes. Adrena removes those barriers and helps people keep going after the first time.

Physical activity

The progress path turns a one-off thrill into a regular sport. Many sports in one place keep you moving all year: on the water in summer, on snow in winter, on the climbing wall in between. Group lessons bring in friends and families.

Sustainability

Most of these sports run on wind, waves and muscle rather than engines. Checking the weather in advance cuts wasted trips, shared rides to lessons save fuel, and gear rental means not everyone has to buy their own.

Local community

Active holidays in Lithuania support small instructor businesses on the coast, by the lakes and in the regions, and the rider community shares experience and inspires others.

What we will measure
  • How many customers continue after the first lesson
  • Hours of active sport per year
  • Share of shared rides and group bookings
  • Income growth for local instructors
