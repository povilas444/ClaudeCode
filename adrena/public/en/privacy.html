Privacy policy

Updated 2026-11-17. The Lithuanian version of this policy is the binding one.

Who we are

Adrena is a student company building an app for finding and booking extreme sports lessons. This policy explains how we handle information when you visit this website and use our forms. Send privacy questions by email to rasauadrena@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

Waitlist form. When you fill in the form we collect your name, email, city (optional) and the sports you are interested in (optional). We only use this data to tell you about the Adrena launch. We do not pass your data to third parties for marketing.

Instructor request form. We collect your name, school or organisation (optional), sport and a contact (email or phone). We only use it to get in touch about an instructor profile on Adrena.

Contact form. We collect your name, email and message. We only use them to reply to you.

App demo. In the demo you can enter a name or upload a video. This stays only in your browser: it is not sent to us, not stored anywhere and disappears when you close or reload the page. The demo has no real payments.

Formspree: form processing service

Our forms run on Formspree (Formspree, Inc., USA). When you submit a form, Formspree receives your data and passes it to us. Formspree stores data on cloud infrastructure in the USA. More information: formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

Formspree complies with the GDPR and can act as a data processor on our behalf. We, Adrena, are the data controller.

How long we keep data

We keep waitlist data until the app launches or until you ask us to delete it. Instructor and contact form data is kept for no longer than 12 months after our last exchange. When the data is no longer needed, we delete it.

When you contact us directly

If you email us or message us on Instagram, we receive your name, contact and message. We only use this to reply and do not share it with anyone. We keep it for no longer than 12 months.

Weather data

The weather on this site comes from the open data of the Lithuanian Hydrometeorological Service (LHMT) at api.meteo.lt, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. The source is shown next to every weather widget. Weather data is public and not linked to personal data.

Cookies and third-party services

We do not use cookies, analytics or ad tracking ourselves. The site fonts load from Google Fonts. These external services may process technical data (IP address, browser type) under their own privacy rules.

Your light or dark theme choice is stored in your browser's localStorage; it is never sent anywhere.

Unsubscribing and deleting data

You can leave the waitlist or ask us to delete your data at any time by emailing rasauadrena@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.

Photos and videos

We only publish our own photos and videos, or ones where the people shown agreed to it. If you recognise yourself and want something removed, write to us.

Your rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to:

  • know what data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate data;
  • ask us to delete your data or restrict how it is processed;
  • object to the processing of your data;
  • receive your data in a portable format (data portability).

Send requests by email to rasauadrena@gmail.com. If you think your data is being handled improperly, you can contact the State Data Protection Inspectorate (vdai.lrv.lt).

The future app

When we launch the real app with sign-up and payments, we will publish a separate, more detailed privacy policy and announce it in advance.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page, with the update date at the top.
