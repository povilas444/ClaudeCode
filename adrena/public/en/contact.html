FAQ

Common questions

When can I download the app?

We are building and testing the prototype right now. Join the waitlist and you will be the first to know. We also post news on Instagram at @adrena_extreme.

Will customers pay to use Adrena?

No. You only pay for the lesson, at the same price the instructor sets. We earn from a commission paid by the instructor or school.

How do you check instructors?

Before joining Adrena an instructor submits certificates and insurance documents. Once we have checked them, we give the "Verified" badge.

What happens if the weather is bad?

The app watches the forecast. If conditions are wrong, you get an alert and can move the lesson for free or get your money back.

Which sports will I find?

We want them all: from kitesurfing, surfing and wakeboarding to skiing, paragliding, skydiving, climbing and diving. We will start with a few and keep adding more.
