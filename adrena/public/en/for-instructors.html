What you get with Adrena

For instructors and schools

More customers, less admin

  • Bookings land straight in your calendar, already paid.
  • Move a day's lessons for every customer with one tap when the weather turns.
  • You only pay the higher commission for new customers we bring you.
  • Adrena is free for customers.
  • A profile with a certificate badge helps customers trust you.
  • The progress system brings customers back to keep learning.

Automatic calendar

Set your open slots and customers book themselves. No more answering every message.

Weather handling

The system watches the forecast and tells customers automatically when conditions change. You confirm or choose an alternative.

Payments

Customers pay when they book. The money is transferred after the lesson through a trusted payment provider.

New customers

Adrena attracts people who have never tried an extreme sport before. For your school that means a new audience.

Business model

How Adrena makes money

Adrena is free for customers: they pay the same price as they would pay the instructor directly. We earn a commission paid by the instructor or school.

Example: a €100 lesson

We only take the higher commission for new customers, so it pays for instructors to run all their bookings through Adrena.

  • Commission15–20% for new customers, 0–5% for returning ones
  • School subscription€20–50 a month for an extended calendar, customer database and reports
  • Gift vouchersSold through Adrena, paid in advance
  • PartnershipsInsurance, gear rental and accommodation

The percentages and amounts are early assumptions that we will refine in talks with instructors.
